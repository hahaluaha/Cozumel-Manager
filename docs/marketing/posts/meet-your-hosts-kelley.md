# Meet Kelley: Nearly 30 Years Hosting on Cozumel

Kelley has been renting on Cozumel since 1997 — nearly thirty years of guests landing on this small Caribbean island and finding a place that already feels like home. Across three properties and 574 guest reviews, one thing shows up again and again: people don't just stay with Kelley, they come back.

## A Host Guests Come Back To

"Wouldn't stay anywhere else in Cozumel," wrote Raniah from Pennsylvania, after Kelley adjusted check-in time to fit her flight. Claire, visiting from Colorado Springs, called her stay "10/10" and said Kelley "was extremely responsive, gave great recommendations, and was thoughtful with all the nice touches she provided." Keith, from Elk Grove, put it simply: "We will definitely stay again. Actually, we already booked it for August."

## Thoughtful Touches, Every Stay

Guests consistently mention the small things that add up. Dana, staying three weeks at Cool Caribbean Views, wrote that Kelley "provided lots of drinking water and ice trays to refill and a coffee maker and coffee to use during my stay along with other kitchen and pantry essentials." At Casa Bohemia — a cozy, newly refurbished townhome two blocks from the oceanfront in the Corpus Christi neighborhood — Keith's family appreciated "the weekly cleaning and the water deliveries." It's the kind of attention that only comes from a host who's still hands-on after decades of doing this.

## A Home Base for Every Kind of Trip

Kelley's guests aren't one type of traveler. Jacqueline, a remote worker from Playa del Carmen, extended her stay after finding the Wi-Fi "perfect." Arica came alone for a dive trip and called the apartment "perfect place for a solo dive trip to Cozumel." John Paul stayed while competing in Ironman Cozumel and called it "an excellent spot to be stationed" for race weekend. Carmen and her family booked a full month at Nah Ha and said Kelley "was quick to respond to all our questions and needs."

## In Her Own Words — and Theirs

Kelley doesn't just list a property and disappear. When a guest ran into an issue, she was there — reviews mention her fumigating within hours of a pest report, adjusting check-in times on short notice, and checking in on guests "several times throughout the stay" with local recommendations, as Kt from Tulsa described.

Kelley wasn't born in Mexico, but she didn't stop at getting by, either — a few years after settling on Cozumel, she made the effort to actually learn Spanish, and can now communicate directly with Spanish-speaking guests and neighbors, not just the English-speaking ones. On an island with plenty of transplants who never do, that's part of what "showing up" has meant for her.

Nearly thirty years in, that's still what defines a stay with Kelley: not a big operation, just a host who knows the island and shows up for her guests.
